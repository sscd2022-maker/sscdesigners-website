Bridge & Highway Structures

Structures along highways and expressways, designed to IRC and MoRTH requirements and coordinated with the alignment and the contractor's construction method.

Reference work: MLFF gantries at eight Delhi locations and at Boarich, Surat; ITS gantries for SICE on the Eastern Peripheral Expressway and Mumbai–Surat highway.

Typical work

  • Bridges, culverts and guide bunds
  • MLFF toll gantries and their pile or open foundations
  • VMS, VSDS and sign board gantries, overhead, cantilever and T-type
  • Toll plaza layouts and toll buildings
  • DPR inputs: bridge and culvert planning, quantities

Deliverables

  • DBR
  • STAAD.Pro model and design report
  • Foundation, base plate and cap plate design
  • GA and fabrication drawings
  • Quantities and BOQ

Codes: IRC:6, IRC:112, IRC:78, IS 800, IS 875 (Part 3), MoRTH Specifications

Industrial & Heavy Equipment Foundations

Foundations and supporting steel for process plants and refineries, designed from vendor loads and the plant layout.

Reference work: 1.2 km two-tier pipe rack at Panipat; pump platforms for 10+ pumps at Mangalore Refinery.

Typical work

  • Machine and equipment foundations
  • Pipe racks, pipe bridges and pipe supports
  • Pump platforms, walkways and access stairs
  • Crane gantry girders and crane sheds

Deliverables

  • Load schedule from vendor data
  • Analysis and design report
  • Foundation and anchor bolt layouts
  • Steel GA and connection details

Codes: IS 456, IS 800, IS 2974, IS 875, IS 1893 (Part 1)

Typical work

  • Tapered portal frames, mezzanines and crane bays
  • Strengthening of existing sheds for new mezzanines

Deliverables

  • DBR and STAAD.Pro model

  • Anchor bolt and foundation drawings

Codes: IS 800, IS 801, IS 875 (Parts 1–3), IS 1893 (Part 1), MBMA where specified

How a job runs

From drawings to issued design

The same sequence on every job, so the client knows what comes next and when.

  1. Inputs and scope

    Architectural and structural drawings, soil report, vendor data and the tender requirements. We list gaps before quoting.

  2. Design basis report

    Codes, materials, loads, combinations and design assumptions agreed with the client before modelling.

  3. Model and design

    STAAD.Pro or SAFE model, member and connection design, foundations from the soil report.

  4. Independent check

    A second engineer checks the model, utilisation, connections and foundations against the DBR.

  5. Issue

    Design report, drawings and BOQ issued at R0, signed by the Chartered Engineer where required.

  6. Revisions and site queries

    Two design revisions included in the fee. Site queries answered during construction.
